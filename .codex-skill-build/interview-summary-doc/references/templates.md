## Candidate Feedback

Use this only when the user explicitly wants feedback that could be shared externally. Keep it constructive and avoid confidential hiring committee language.

```md
# Interview Feedback

## Strengths Observed
- [Job-relevant positive observation]
- [Job-relevant positive observation]

## Areas To Strengthen
- [Constructive, actionable point]
- [Constructive, actionable point]

## Suggested Focus
- [What to practice or clarify next]
```
